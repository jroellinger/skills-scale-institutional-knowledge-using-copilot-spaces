# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder brings together the guides, roles, and practices OctoAcme uses to manage cross-functional projects.

## Project management process

OctoAcme's process moves work through a lightweight, iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by validating the problem, defining measurable outcomes, identifying stakeholders, and deciding whether to proceed. They then plan scope and shippable increments, prioritize the backlog, agree on milestones and responsibilities, and identify dependencies and risks. During execution, teams track work on a project board, communicate progress and blockers, and build, test, review, and iterate in small increments. Before release, they confirm acceptance criteria and quality checks, deploy, verify the result, and communicate the release. Finally, teams close the loop with a retrospective, assigning and tracking improvement actions.
